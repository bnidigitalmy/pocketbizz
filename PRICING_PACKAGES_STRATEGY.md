## 💡 PRICING PSYCHOLOGY

### Why These Prices Work:

**1. Anchor Effect** 
- Premium at RM159 makes Pro (RM89) look reasonable
- Most will choose Pro (60-70% of paid users)

**2. Value Perception**
- Vendor claims alone worth RM200-500/month
- Reseller network can add RM500-1000/month revenue
- System pays for itself 5-10x

**3. Easy Decision Making**
- Trial → Basic (obvious upgrade for RM29)
- Basic → Pro (vendor claims is must-have)
- Pro → Premium (for serious businesses only)

**4. Local Market Fit**
- Competitor: Foodics RM299 (too expensive!)
- Competitor: StoreHub RM149 (still high)
- PocketBizz Pro RM89 = 40% cheaper + Malaysian features
